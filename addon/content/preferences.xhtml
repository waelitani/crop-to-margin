Crop to Margin Finds the ink on a PDF's pages, hides the paper around it, and zooms in so the text block fills the reader instead of the page it was printed on. This is the same switch as the crop button in the reader toolbar: whatever you leave it at is what the next PDF opens with. The crop is measured from the body of the document. An index or a plate that sets type wider keeps whatever margin it needs. Tuning A cropped page sits in a smaller box than pdf.js measures it in, so continuous scrolling drifts. Turning the pages avoids it. Pair this with fitting the whole page below if you want nothing to scroll at all. Remembered crops Every PDF is measured once; the crop is reused instantly the next time you open it.
